3. THE CORE USPs: SIMPLE TERMS VS DEEP-TECH
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USP 1: MULTI-CONDITION RETINAL BIOMARKERS
       (Diabetic Retinopathy + Glaucoma CDR + Cardiovascular Age & Stroke Risk)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Layman's Simple Terms:
  The retina is the ONLY place in the human body where you can look directly at 
  living blood vessels and nerves without cutting a person open.
  Instead of just checking for "diabetic damage", Netra uses that one photo to 
  perform a 3-in-1 total health check:
  1. Diabetic Retinopathy (Grades 0 to 4).
  2. Glaucoma Susceptibility (Checks if the optic nerve is being crushed by pressure).
  3. Cardiovascular & Biological Vascular Age: Tells a 42-year-old patient if their 
     blood vessels look like a 58-year-old's, warning them of stroke and heart attack 
     risks years before a cardiac event occurs.

* Tech / Under The Hood:
  - Deep convolutional feature extractor combined with segmentation heads.
  - Cup-to-Disc Ratio (CDR) measurement: Segments the optic disc and optic cup; 
    a vertical CDR > 0.6 flags high glaucoma risk.
  - Retinal Microvascular Morphometry: Computes the Arteriolar-to-Venular Ratio (AVR) 
    and vessel tortuosity index to quantify microvascular hypertension and systemic 
    endothelial damage.
  - Biological Age Predictor: Regressed from vascular caliber and branching geometry, 
    estimating a 5-Year MACE (Major Adverse Cardiovascular Event) risk percentile.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USP 2: AI CATARACT DEHAZING & OPTICAL VESSEL RESTORATION
       (Solving the #1 Hardware Bottleneck in Rural India)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Layman's Simple Terms:
  In rural villages, 70% of elderly diabetic patients also have cloudy cataracts. 
  Furthermore, health workers use cheap smartphone lens attachments that cause 
  glare, blur, and yellowish fog. Most medical AI models fail and say "Ungradable".
  Netra includes an embedded optical restoration filter that digitally "cleans 
  the fog" and enhances blood vessels, turning an unusable murky photo into a 
  crystal-clear diagnostic image.

* Tech / Under The Hood:
  - Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) applied selectively 
    over the green channel (where hemoglobin absorbs light most strongly).
  - Multi-scale Retinex (MSR) illumination-correction algorithm to strip out 
    corneal cataract scattering and vignetting.
  - Image Quality Assessment (IQA) gatekeeper measuring Laplacian variance 
    (blur score) and histogram dynamic range (underexposure/overexposure detection) 
    before feeding into downstream classification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USP 3: OFFLINE-FIRST SYNC & CRYPTOGRAPHIC QR "DIAGNOSTIC PASSPORT"
       (Zero-Connectivity Rural Health Architecture)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Layman's Simple Terms:
  If an ASHA worker goes into a remote valley with zero 4G or Wi-Fi, Netra doesn't 
  crash or stop working. The worker can scan 50 patients offline. 
  When back in network range, one tap syncs everything with zero duplicate records, 
  even if the worker clicks "Sync" multiple times or the connection drops halfway.
  Additionally, Netra generates a "Diagnostic Passport" QR code that the patient 
  takes to the city hospital. The city doctor can scan this QR code on ANY phone 
  with NO INTERNET and read the patient's verified AI diagnosis instantly!

* Tech / Under The Hood:
  - Client-side decentralized UUID v4 generation: Devices assign primary keys 
    locally without consulting the central database.
  - Idempotent Batch Sync (POST /api/v1/sync/offline): Backend indexes `client_uuid` 
    with unique constraints; duplicate uploads are safely skipped while establishing 
    relational foreign keys in Spring Boot/PostgreSQL.
  - Cryptographic QR Diagnostic Passport: Packages patient demographics, screening 
    findings, and referral reason into a self-contained, base64-encoded, digitally 
    signed JWT/HMAC token. Verifiable entirely offline by any standard QR reader.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USP 4: AYUSHMAN BHARAT (ABDM / ABHA) & HL7-FHIR NATIONAL HEALTH BRIDGE
       (Ready for Pan-India Government Deployment)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Layman's Simple Terms:
  Netra is not a private island. It is built to seamlessly plug into the Indian 
  Government's Ayushman Bharat health network. Every scan links to the citizen's 
  14-digit ABHA ID (Ayushman Bharat Health Account). The diagnosis is automatically 
  saved to their national digital health locker, so government district hospitals 
  have access to the record the second the patient walks through the door.

* Tech / Under The Hood:
  - Interoperability via the international HL7 FHIR standard (Fast Healthcare 
    Interoperability Resources).
  - Transforms screening outputs into a FHIR-compliant `DiagnosticReport` and 
    `Observation` resource bundle.
  - Pre-structured for ABDM Milestones (M1, M2, M3): ABHA issuance, consent-based 
    record linking, and HIP/HIU (Health Information Provider/User) telemetry.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USP 5: MATHEMATICAL GEOSPATIAL REFERRAL ROUTING
       (Connecting Diagnosis to Life-Saving Care)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Layman's Simple Terms:
  Diagnosing a patient is useless if they don't know where to go. If Netra finds 
  severe disease, it immediately calculates the closest government or private eye 
  hospital that actually has an ophthalmologist and surgical facilities, providing 
  exact distance in kilometers, doctor name, and contact details.

* Tech / Under The Hood:
  - High-performance in-memory Haversine spherical trigonometric calculation:
    d = 2R * arcsin(sqrt(sin^2(dlat/2) + cos(lat1)*cos(lat2)*sin^2(dlon/2)))
  - Computes instant distance vectors without expensive third-party Google Maps 
    API overhead or external latency. Filters by specialist tier and facility type.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USP 6: AUTOMATED WHATSAPP PATIENT COMPLIANCE ENGINE
       (Closing the Referral Gap)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Layman's Simple Terms:
  Over 50% of rural patients who are told to visit a hospital simply forget or 
  delay until they go blind. Netra automatically triggers an urgent WhatsApp alert 
  in simple language to the patient’s or family member's phone, giving them their 
  appointment timeline, nearest hospital address, and a link to their QR passport.

* Tech / Under The Hood:
  - Asynchronous event-driven notification state machine (QUEUED -> SENT -> DELIVERED).
  - Webhook delivery verification architecture, ready for integration with Meta 
    WhatsApp Business Cloud API or Gupshup/Twilio gateways.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USP 7: CONTINUOUS LEARNING FLYWHEEL (HOSPITAL CONTRIBUTION PORTAL)
       (AI That Gets Smarter Over Time)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Layman's Simple Terms:
  Most AI systems never improve once deployed. Netra has a built-in portal where 
  ophthalmologists at district hospitals can upload verified hospital clinical 
  scans and confirmed diagnoses. Admins review and approve these, creating a clean 
  retraining pipeline so the AI constantly adapts to diverse Indian demographics.

* Tech / Under The Hood:
  - Multi-tenant contribution ingestion pipeline (manual single-case upload or 
    bulk ZIP archive ingestion).
  - Multi-stage curation queue (PENDING_REVIEW -> APPROVED -> RETRAINING_DATASET) 
    preventing data poisoning while building a localized clinical data moat.
